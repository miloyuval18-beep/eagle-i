High-Value Area Permits
Real, current Houston building permits · sourced weekly from the city's own published reports
🌐 Real data — with two honest limits
Pulled directly from the Houston Permitting Center's weekly published permit reports (real addresses, real permit types, updated weekly — not real-time). No owner name is included in this data, only the property address — which is exactly what's needed for postal mail (addressed to "Property Owner" or "Current Resident" is standard and doesn't require a name). "High-value" areas are a curated reference list of well-known Houston neighborhoods with approximate median home values from public real-estate reporting, not live appraisal data — zip codes outside that list still show real permits, just without a value label.
Click "Load Permits" to pull this week's real Houston permit data

My name is [FOUNDER_NAME]. I'm reaching out from [COMPANY_NAME], serving [SERVICE_AREA].

You can reach me directly at [PHONE] or [EMAIL]. We'd be glad to answer any questions about your property — no obligation, no pitch.

[FOUNDER_NAME] · [COMPANY_NAME]
Why mail, not phone/email
Cold-calling or texting people from public permit records without consent risks TCPA/Do-Not-Call violations. Postal mail to a permit address isn't subject to that law, which is why this tool surfaces addresses, not phone numbers or emails.
